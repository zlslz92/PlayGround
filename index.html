FIELD GUIDE

How to disappear

Specimen: Vitreus pudor
01

Read the ground

Each patch wants a shape. Press Q to switch between moving and disguising. In disguise, hold still while the blend meter fills.

02

Touch a landmark

Walk against a fern, boulder, mushroom or crystal to copy its shape. Press E near one to copy it deliberately.

03

Paint the slime

Pick a colour, then drag on the slime portrait. Match ground (F) samples the whole palette beneath you. Texture counts as much as hue.

04

Use the walls

Near a stone, press Space to cling. Climbing flattens you into the rock face and disguises you as stone. Press again to drop.

05

Outwit the warden

Movement breaks camouflage. Dash with Shift; the lantern can hear a hurried slime. Boulders block its sight, and a crowded grove hides you better than empty ground.

06

Take a turn as warden

As Warden, sweep the beam with the mouse or your direction. E pulses for nearby heartbeats; Space tags a slime at close range. Tag all five before the moon peaks.
